Budget

A personal budget for iPhone that keeps your money where you can see it — on your phone, and, if you want, in your own Google Sheets.

What it does

  • Record spending and income in a few taps, in any currency, converted at that day's rate.
  • See where the month is going: categories, limits, recurring payments and a forecast to the end of the month.
  • Savings goals, debts, shared expenses and a shopping list.
  • Optional two-way sync with Google Sheets: one spreadsheet per month, in a format you can read and edit yourself.
  • Import a bank statement (CSV) and export a full backup at any time.

Your data

There is no Budget server and no account to create. Your entries live on your iPhone. If you connect Google Sheets, the app talks to Google directly and can open only the spreadsheets it created or that you choose for it. Details are in the privacy policy.
