Privacy policy

Budget for iPhone · effective 13 November 2026

Budget is built so that your financial data stays with you. The developer runs no server, collects no analytics and receives none of your data.

What is stored, and where

  • On your iPhone. Entries, categories, limits, goals, debts, shared-expense rules and the shopping list are stored in the app's own storage on your device and in its home-screen widget.
  • In your Google account, only if you connect it. The app reads and writes your monthly spreadsheets in Google Sheets. It talks to Google directly from your phone; nothing passes through the developer.
  • Files you export. A backup or CSV export is saved wherever you choose, and only when you ask for it.

Google account access

Budget asks Google for one permission, drive.file: access only to the spreadsheets the app created itself. It cannot see any other file in your Google Drive.

  • The data is used only to show your budget in the app and to keep it in step with those spreadsheets. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone and not used to train AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the iPhone Keychain, on this device only.
  • Signing out in Settings → Google Sheets deletes the tokens and asks Google to revoke the app's access. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Budget's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other network requests

  • Exchange rates. For entries in a foreign currency the app downloads public daily rates from the open currency-api project (via cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.dev). The request contains a currency code and a date — no amounts and nothing about you.
  • Purchases. Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; the app only learns whether Premium is active.

As with any internet request, these services see your device's IP address. The app adds no identifiers of its own.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Your spreadsheets stay in your Google Drive, where you can delete them like any other file.

Children

Budget is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: ivanhozeev357@gmail.com

Политика конфиденциальности

Budget для iPhone · действует с 13 ноября 2026

У приложения нет сервера и нет аналитики. Разработчик не получает ваших данных.

  • На iPhone хранятся записи, категории, лимиты, цели, долги, правила общих расходов и список покупок — в памяти приложения и его виджета.
  • В вашем аккаунте Google, только если вы его подключили: приложение читает и пишет таблицы месяцев напрямую с телефона.
  • Доступ к Google — одно разрешение drive.file: только таблицы, которые приложение создало само. Остальной Google Диск приложению не виден.
  • Данные из Google используются только для показа бюджета и синхронизации; они не продаются, не передаются третьим лицам, не используются для рекламы и обучения ИИ. Использование соответствует Google API Services User Data Policy, включая требования Limited Use.
  • Токены входа лежат в Keychain только на этом устройстве. «Выйти» удаляет их и отзывает доступ у Google; отозвать его можно и на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Курсы валют загружаются из открытого проекта currency-api (cdn.jsdelivr.net, currency-api.pages.dev): в запросе только код валюты и дата.
  • Premium продаётся через App Store; оплату обрабатывает Apple.
  • Удаление приложения стирает всё, что оно хранило на iPhone. Таблицы остаются в вашем Google Диске.
